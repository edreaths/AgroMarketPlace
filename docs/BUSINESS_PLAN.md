# Executive Summary

AgroMarketPlace is a digital agricultural marketplace designed to connect farmers, wholesalers, retailers, agro-businesses, logistics providers, and consumers through one secure online platform.

The platform enables users to buy and sell agricultural products efficiently while reducing middlemen, increasing transparency, and improving market access.

Our goal is to digitize agricultural commerce across Africa, beginning with Nigeria.

# Problem Statement

Agricultural trading still faces major challenges including:

- Limited market access
- Poor price transparency
- Fraudulent transactions
- Difficult logistics
- Lack of trust between buyers and sellers
- Poor inventory management
- Limited digital adoption

---
